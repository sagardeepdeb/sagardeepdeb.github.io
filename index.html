Sagar Deep Deb

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Petroleum and Energy Studies (UPES), working at the intersection of machine learning, deep learning, and healthcare. My research focuses on biomedical signal and image analysis, ventilator dyssynchrony, and clinical decision-support systems. I hold a Ph.D. (2024) in Electrical Engineering from IIT Patna and previously completed a postdoctoral fellowship at IIT Bombay before joining UPES.
